The forensic tooling below is NOT redistributed with this solution. Each is
downloaded or built at acquisition or investigation time, onto instances in the
deploying customer's own account, at the pinned versions recorded in the SSM
documents under source/ssm-documents. It is listed here so that operators know
which licences apply to the tools their evidence passes through:

LiME (Linux Memory Extractor), pinned at tag v1.12.0 from
  https://github.com/jtsylve/LiME, under the GPL-2.0 license. Compiled into a
  kernel module and loaded on the instance under investigation. Not linked into,
  distributed with, or derived from this solution's own code.
AVML (Acquire Volatile Memory for Linux), pinned at release v0.20.0 from
  https://github.com/microsoft/avml, under the MIT license. Downloaded as a
  released binary and verified against a per-architecture SHA256 digest.
Volatility 3, pinned at tag v2.28.0 from
  https://github.com/volatilityfoundation/volatility3, under the Volatility
  Software License (VSL), a modified BSD-style license.
dwarf2json, pinned at tag v0.9.0 from
  https://github.com/volatilityfoundation/dwarf2json, under the MIT license.
plaso / log2timeline, run from the log2timeline/plaso container image pinned by
  digest in the analysis AMI, under the Apache-2.0 license.
pefile, pinned at version 2024.8.26 from PyPI, under the MIT license.
winpmem, from https://github.com/Velocidex/WinPmem, under the Apache-2.0
  license. Used for Windows memory acquisition.
